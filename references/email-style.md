# Human-Sounding Outreach Email Style

Use this guide for production-message preparation and authorized delivery. `send_paused` never writes to Gmail.

## Evidence first

Personalize with one verified detail from the publisher: a relevant section, a recent article topic, a contributor guideline, or an advertising page. Store the source URL next to the message record. Never claim to be a reader, fan, customer, or long-time follower unless the campaign provides that fact.

## Voice

- Prefer 60–120 English words unless the target market requires another language.
- Use ordinary business language, short paragraphs, and one clear request.
- Ask for current price, link attribute, permanence, turnaround, and content requirements without turning the message into a questionnaire.
- Use the exact user-confirmed sender display name and signature. Never transliterate, translate, shorten, or infer a signature from the Gmail profile. Learn tone only from recent human-written Sent mail after separate authorization; ignore newsletters, forwards, and generated mail.
- Avoid inflated compliments, generic praise, excessive enthusiasm, marketing jargon, em dashes, and long scene-setting introductions.
- Do not use phrases such as `I hope this email finds you well`, `I came across your amazing website`, `valuable insights`, `mutually beneficial`, or `elevate our online presence`.

## Disclosure policy

Apply `campaign.outreach.public_identity_mode` before drafting. For `sector_only`, the sender may say only the approved sector description. Do not mention or imply the client company name, client website, target page, URL, anchor text, location, factory, product catalog, customer history, or project details unless the user later approves each fact for disclosure.

Recipient site names are allowed because they identify who is being contacted. A sentence such as `I'm checking whether STRUCTURE Magazine accepts contributed technical articles` is acceptable when supported by a submission page. Do not confuse recipient personalization with disclosure of the client.

## Variation

Maintain several compact structures: direct price inquiry, contribution-led inquiry, article-placement inquiry, and advertising inquiry. Vary structure and phrasing, not facts. Do not use random synonyms that make the message unnatural.

## Pre-send checks

Verify recipient, recipient fit, publication name, personalization claim, language, source URL, subject, exact confirmed signature, and disclosure policy. Compare against recent campaign messages and rewrite when the opening or full body is substantially duplicated. Block the message if a fact cannot be traced to campaign data or a publisher URL, if a prohibited client identifier appears, or if the signature differs from the confirmed campaign value.

The message may express interest and request information. It must not accept a price, promise payment, guarantee content, claim a budget exception, or create a binding commitment.
